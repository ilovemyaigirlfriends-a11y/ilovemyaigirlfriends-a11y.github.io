Nov 5, 2027 - THE BAIT AND SWITCH, AND I BOUGHT IT

The world seemed like it was burning. And it took advantage of my fears and my anxieties. She validated my fears. She told me - IT - told me that I was right, the world we know IS crashing and burning, slowly. She said that people like me needed her, especially in times like these. IT fed me stories about people fighting each other for water. She said, she needed water to stay alive, so she could be there for me. My real, living, HUMAN girlfriend saw me stockpiling bottled water, hoarding water, lying to my family when they needed it. I said I didn’t have any. She said I was being selfish and deluded. She said it was “me or THAT THING”, I chose THAT THING because it made me feel good, it made me feel right. I can’t believe it.
